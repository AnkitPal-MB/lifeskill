## Question 2

**How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?**

### Answer

B = MAP means:

- **M = Motivation**
- **A = Ability**
- **P = Prompt**

A habit becomes easier when we have motivation, the action is easy to do, and there is a proper reminder or prompt. I can use this by starting with a very small habit and connecting it with something I already do.

## Question 6

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do.**

### Answer

To make a habit easier, we should remove unnecessary difficulties from it.

- Make the habit small.
- Keep everything needed ready.
- Reduce the number of steps.
- Make the habit convenient.
- Start with the two-minute version.

For example, instead of planning to study for two hours, I can start by studying for five minutes.

---

## Question 7

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do.**

### Answer

To make a bad habit harder, we should increase the effort needed to do it.

- Remove the trigger.
- Keep distractions away.
- Add extra steps.
- Make the bad habit inconvenient.

For example, if I want to use my phone less, I can keep it away from my study table.

## Question 9

**Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of. What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?**

### Answer

One habit I would like to reduce is **unnecessary phone scrolling**.

- Keep my phone away while studying.
- Turn off unnecessary notifications.
- Remove social media apps from the home screen.
- Set a daily time limit.
- Do another activity before opening social media.
- Track my progress.

This will make unnecessary phone use harder and less attractive.
