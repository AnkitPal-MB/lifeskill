# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

**Answer:**  
Grit means staying committed to a goal and continuing to work hard even when things are difficult or progress is slow.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

**Answer:**  
A growth mindset means believing that our abilities can improve through effort, practice, learning, and feedback. Mistakes help us learn and improve.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

**Answer:**

- **Internal Locus of Control:** Believing that our actions and decisions can influence our results.
- **Key point:** Focus on what you can control instead of blaming external factors.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

**Key points:**

- Embrace challenges.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Accept feedback.
- Keep trying after failure.
- Focus on effort and improvement.
- Learn from successful people.
- Replace **“I can’t do it”** with **“I can’t do it yet.”**
- Stay open to learning.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

- Set small learning goals.
- Practice consistently.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Ask for feedback.
- Try challenging coding problems.
- Learn something new every day.
- Track my improvement instead of comparing myself with others.
- Stay patient when learning difficult topics.
- Reflect on what I learned after completing a task.
