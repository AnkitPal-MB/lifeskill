# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? (1 line)

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a topic in simple words, like you are teaching someone else.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active Mode:** This is when you are fully focused on a task, like solving a coding problem or learning a new concept.
- **Diffused Mode:** This is when your mind is relaxed. During this time, your brain connects ideas and helps you understand things better.

# Learning Principles in the Bootcamp

- Give your full attention while learning or coding.
- Programming becomes better with focus and regular practice.
- Improve your concentration every day.
- Work seriously during study time and relax after finishing your work.
- Keep your phone on silent and turn off unnecessary notifications.
- Avoid using social media while studying.
- Track your study time to improve your productivity.

---

# How to Learn Software Concepts

- Understand where the concept is used.
- Explain the concept in your own words.
- Practice it by writing code or creating a small project.
- Set a target to complete the practice or assignment.

---

# Important Points

- Use deadlines to manage your learning.
- Focus on understanding instead of memorizing.
- Learn in your own way and make the topic easy for yourself.
- If you can teach a concept to someone else, you understand it well.
- It is better to master a few important skills than know many topics only at a basic level.

---

# How Trainees Are Evaluated

- Can you explain concepts clearly?
- Can you write clean and correct code?
- Do you complete your work on time?
- Do you communicate when you face problems?
- Do you express your ideas clearly?
