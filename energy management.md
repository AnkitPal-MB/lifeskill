# Energy Management — Questions & Answers

## Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

- Listening to music
- Going for a walk
- Watching something I like
- Spending time with friends or family
- Taking a short nap
- Doing my hobbies

## Question 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

- When I have too much work to finish.
- When I have a tight deadline.
- When I am learning something difficult.
- When I have exams or interviews.
- When I overthink about a problem.

## Question 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

- I feel energetic and motivated.
- I enjoy what I am doing.
- I can focus easily on the task.
- I feel curious to learn or try something new.
- I don't feel forced to do the activity.

## Question 4: Paraphrase the "Sleep is Your Superpower" video in your own words.

- Good sleep is important for both the body and brain.
- Sleep helps improve memory and learning.
- Lack of sleep can affect our mood and concentration.
- Good sleep supports the immune system.
- Sleep helps the body recover and repair itself.
- We should give proper importance to our sleep routine.

## Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Sleep and wake up at a fixed time.
- Avoid using the phone before sleeping.
- Avoid caffeine late in the day.
- Keep the room dark and comfortable.
- Avoid heavy meals before sleeping.
- Exercise regularly.
- Relax before going to bed.
- Get enough sleep every night.

## Question 6: Paraphrase the "Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise" video.

- Exercise improves our brain function.
- It helps improve memory and learning.
- Exercise can reduce stress and anxiety.
- It improves our mood and makes us feel more energetic.
- Regular exercise helps protect the brain as we grow older.
- Exercise can improve focus and concentration.
- It also helps the brain create and maintain healthy connections.

## Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Start with 15–20 minutes of exercise every day.
- Take short walks during breaks.
- Use stairs instead of the lift.
- Play a sport that I enjoy.
- Set a fixed time for exercise.
- Exercise with friends to stay motivated.
- Slowly increase the duration instead of doing too much at once.
